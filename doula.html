From policy decision to completed human-approved residual

LensDNA executes the browser, document, coordination, and evidence work that remains after Medicaid or commercial coverage decisions make doula care reimbursable. This surface demonstrates one narrow residual loop — credentialing, coverage confirmation, and outcomes documentation — not a general care platform or clinical system.

Trust boundary: Synthetic demonstration only. No real patient, doula, or health-plan data. No endorsement, partnership, integration, or customer relationship with any named organization is claimed or implied. LensDNA does not make clinical determinations. Irreversible actions require explicit human approval. Residual shape is relevant to the 26 states + DC with Medicaid doula reimbursement (as of 2026).

What the residual layer does

Policy and platform layers can open reimbursement. They often leave fragmented operational work: MCO credentialing portals, coverage confirmation, visit documentation, outcomes reporting, and claim-ready packets.

  1. Receive the enrollment / coverage decision and preserve case context
  2. Classify the operational residual required
  3. Navigate the relevant synthetic MCO / plan portal
  4. Stage credentialing fields, coverage confirmation, and documentation
  5. Pause before any irreversible submit or external action
  6. Present the complete action set for human review
  7. Record approved / rejected / amended actions + audit trail
