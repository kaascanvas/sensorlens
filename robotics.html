From robot event to completed human-approved workflow

LensDNA executes the browser, phone, documentation, coordination, and evidence work that remains after an education or therapy robot creates an event. This surface demonstrates one narrow residual loop — not a general learning platform, avatar product, or robot-control system.

Trust boundary: Synthetic demonstration only. No real student, patient, teacher, therapist, or family data. No endorsement, partnership, integration, or customer relationship with any named robotics or education provider is claimed or implied. LensDNA does not make educational or clinical determinations. Irreversible actions require explicit human approval.

What the residual layer does

Robots and intelligent education platforms can create presence, interaction, instruction, observation, and events. They often leave fragmented operational work: portal entry, staff notification, family coordination, support tickets, case notes, escalation, scheduling, evidence, and audit records.

  1. Receive the robot/avatar event and preserve case context
  2. Classify the operational follow-up required
  3. Navigate the relevant synthetic portal or support surface
  4. Stage fields, notifications, and evidence packets
  5. Pause before any external communication or irreversible log
  6. Present the complete action set for human review
  7. Record approved / rejected / amended actions + audit trail
